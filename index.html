What the live sources can and cannot tell you

Open Beauty Facts is a community-maintained product-label database. PubChem provides chemical identity information, not a cosmetic safety verdict. EU CosIng is an informative ingredient database and does not itself approve an ingredient for use. Customer complaints and adverse events are not comprehensively available through a free public API here, so BlushCheck links to searches rather than inventing review counts. A real safety determination also needs concentrations, product type, exposure and a qualified product safety assessment.
